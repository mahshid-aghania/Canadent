The AFK exam, officially called the Assessment of Fundamental Knowledge, is one of the most important steps for international dentists who want to become licensed in Canada.<\/p>\n<\/p>\n

It is part of the official NDEB equivalency process for graduates of non-accredited dental programs. According to the NDEB, the AFK tests biomedical science and applied clinical science knowledge, and a passing grade is required before candidates can move forward to the next examinations in the equivalency process.<\/p>\n<\/p>\n
